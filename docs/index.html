A few honest field notes

The map is an archive, not a live wildlife tracker. Locations are approximate and sightings may be historical. The starter collection contains examples, not observations made by you. Character art is AI-created and prepared in advance; live identification and live character generation are not connected here.

Your photos stay with you

Photos you add are resized and stored in this browser. They are never uploaded by this app. Optional location is used only when you choose it. Map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap; photo credits and licenses appear on every archive entry. View all credits and licenses ↗
