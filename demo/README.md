# Nature Relay demo

[Watch the 68-second demo](https://midori11111111.github.io/nature-relay/media/nature-relay-demo.mp4).

The map, photo-import/manual-species-choice, and Journal segments show the actual iPhone app. The De Anza sweet-alyssum camera segment is visibly marked CONCEPT DEMO: its recognition, character overlay, Collect and Journal result are simulated. Prepared AI illustrations are used by the app; this does not demonstrate live exact-species identification or runtime artwork generation.

Source photographs retain their credits in the slides/video: rappman (iNaturalist observation80177721), Bruce Bailey (363792117), William Walker (250218495), and Annette Herz (108094155), CC BY4.0. Individual links and licenses are in Resources/CaliforniaCatalog/catalog.json. The project owner supplied the campus footage for this presentation. Character images are original Nature Relay project artwork.

The video is silent, 1920×1080,30fps,68seconds. Four complete short app/concept recordings total53seconds; the remaining seconds are the opening, character, sharing and closing slides. It incorporates the final character choices. Native slides are presented separately with manual advances.
